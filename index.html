Things I Am Currently Thinking About

  • Amplifier versus "Sloplifer" I know this has been widely observed and discussed, but it has been on my mind lately. I have found a workflow for using LLMs as an amplifier in my workflow for areas that I consider myself a domain-level expert. This is largely productive because I know how to steer the interaction due to my background knowledge and call our methodological mistakes in responses when they occur. I worry that people that use LLMs for uses far out of their domain expertise will get arbitrarily bad responses and the upstream consequences of this will be far worse than the spread of "AI slop".
