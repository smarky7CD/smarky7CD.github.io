Things I Am Currently Thinking About

  • Information Overload Another possible hazard with AI/LLM usage that I have recently been thinking about is what I am calling "Information Overload". LLM generated code makes it very easy to generate dashboards, reports, etc. with a ton of information. While this can be very useful, it also creates the issue where it can be harder to separate signal from noise.
