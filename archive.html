August 2026

Things I Am Currently Thinking About

  • Amplifier versus "Sloplifer" I know this has been widely observed and discussed, but it has been on my mind lately. I have found a workflow for using LLMs as an amplifier in my workflow for areas that I consider myself a domain-level expert. This is largely productive because I know how to steer the interaction due to my background knowledge and call ourtmethodological mistakes in responses when they occur. I worry that people that use LLMs for uses far out of their domain expertise will get arbitrarily bad responses and the upstream consequences of this will be far worse than the spread of standard "AI slop".

Things I Am Currently Thinking About

  • Benefits to Adding Complexity to Models (2x) I am still actively working here At my day job I work with market data and at the particular time scales that are currently pertinent us, this data is very noisy. Trying to craft overly complex models leads to overfitting and often unstable results. For people working at lower timescales, e.g. HFT shops, it is clear that they are leveraging more complex models -- quite profitably. I am attempting to scour the literature (both academic and releases from these shops) to develop a more complete picture of what types of models are being used and where they are being used. This is an attempt to both release this information as a blog post and to see if any of these ideas are relevant for my work.

January 2026

Things I Am Currently Thinking About

  • Maximizing Human Flourishing through a New Process for Basic Research and Development I was recently doing a thought experiment about what I would do if I have a large amount of capital I was able to deploy. This is what I came up with (albeit, in a cursory manner). I want to maximize human flourishing by funding high impact research and development. I imagine this is just a function of identifying potentially high impact individuals and their teams and letting them work on whatever they want with little interference The sustainable structure around this is to build a development company that has some profit sharing agreement with the independent research teams that allow us to monetize the research both on short and long term time horizons. This both incentivizes the development phase of research and the sustained goal of funding basic research.
  • Prediction Markets I originally became interested in prediction markets a number of years ago when I heard about an early Ethereum project called Augur. I then read a good amount Furturachy from Robin Hanson. I am remiss that I think current prediction market implementations do not match the theory -- they might just be a "Sportsbook++". This does not mean, however, that there is not anything interesting there from an intellectual curiosity perspective.
